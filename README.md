### Aaron Clayton

**UX-minded systems engineer. I work where the interface meets the infrastructure — designing the parts people touch, automating the parts they shouldn't have to, and applying AI only where it genuinely earns its place.**

---

I design the parts of a system people actually use, build the infrastructure underneath it,
and automate the manual work once it's understood and stable.

**User experience** — systems people don't fight. The interface is where a system succeeds or
fails, so I design for the person using it on a bad day, not the demo.

**Systems management** — infrastructure that stays up, stays observable and stays cheap. I care
more about what happens at 3am than what happens in the pitch.

**Automation & applied AI** — manual work removed, and AI only where it earns its place. Good
automation is boring in the best way: it runs, it's idempotent, and nobody has to think about it.
